Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains how subscription cancellations and refunds work for XverseMovies. All amounts are in Indian Rupees (INR).

1. Cancelling Your Subscription

You can cancel your subscription any time from Settings → Plan & Billing → Cancel Membership. Once cancelled:

  • Your plan reverts to the free Basic tier.
  • You retain access to your current plan's features until the end of the billing period you've already paid for.
  • No further charges will be made after cancellation.

2. Refunds

Because subscription access is granted immediately upon successful payment, payments are generally non-refundable once a billing cycle has started — similar to standard practice for digital subscription services.

Exceptions where a refund may be considered:

  • Duplicate charge — you were charged more than once for the same billing cycle due to a technical error.
  • Failed upgrade — payment was deducted but your plan was not upgraded within 24 hours, and our support team is unable to resolve it manually.
  • Unauthorized transaction — a payment was made without your authorization (subject to verification).

To request a refund under these circumstances, contact us within 7 days of the transaction via our Contact page, including your registered email and the payment reference/order ID.

3. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method (UPI, card, or netbanking) via Cashfree Payments, and typically reflect within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.

4. Failed / Pending Payments

If a payment fails or remains "pending" on Cashfree's checkout and no plan upgrade occurs, no amount is retained — any amount debited due to a failed transaction is auto-reversed by Cashfree/your bank, typically within 5–7 business days, without needing to contact us.

5. Contact

For any billing question or refund request, reach out via our Contact page.
